About Me

Data only becomes valuable when someone turns it into a clear answer. I am a second-year Data Science student and aspiring Data Analyst who takes messy, raw datasets and turns them into insights people can act on, whether that means understanding sales performance, spotting trends, or presenting the numbers in a dashboard that anyone can read at a glance.

My approach is to start with the question, not the tool. I clean and prepare data with Python, Pandas and Power Query, explore it to find the patterns that matter, and communicate the results through Power BI, Excel and Matplotlib visuals. My studies in Data Science give me a solid foundation in statistics and analysis, and I back it up with hands-on projects and a Power BI course certificate.

What sets me apart is the technical background behind my analysis. As a software developer, I have built business applications, including one that is currently in use by a client, so I understand how data is captured, stored and structured long before it reaches a spreadsheet. My networking and IT support skills also let me solve the technical problems around the data instead of waiting for someone else to fix them.
