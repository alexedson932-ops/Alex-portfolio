Project Overview

Helping organize common hostel administration tasks.

The Hostel Management System was developed as an academic project to bring important hostel administration tasks into one system. It covers student registration, room and bed management, hostel payments, resident information, and income reports.

The project provided practical experience in server-side web development, working with a database, and organizing application features around an administrative workflow.
