Phone Credit Manager

A mobile application developed for a phone shop to help manage phones sold on credit and keep related customer and business records organized.

Completed · Currently in use

Project Overview

A practical solution built for a real business need.

Phone Credit Manager was developed for a client who operates a phone shop that sells devices on credit. The application helps the business keep its credit-sale information organized and makes it easier to refer back to relevant records.

The project gave me practical experience in building a mobile application, connecting it to a backend service, and delivering a solution that is already being used by the intended business.
