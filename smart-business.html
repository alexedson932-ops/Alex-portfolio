Project Overview

About the project

Smart Business Analytics is a business management application developed to help businesses organize their daily operations through technology.

The project was inspired by research and discussions with business owners to understand some of the challenges they face when managing their businesses. The goal was to develop a digital solution that could help address these challenges and make business management more organized.

The application is available as an Android application, alongside a website.
